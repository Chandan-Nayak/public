## 2025

### ⭐ Highly Recommended

#### Atomic Habits
- **Author:** James Clear
- **Finished:** 2025-11-15
- **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Genre:** Self-improvement
- **Summary:** Practical guide to building good habits and breaking bad ones through small, incremental changes
- **Key Takeaways:**
  - Focus on systems, not goals
  - The 1% improvement rule compounds over time
  - Make it obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying
  - Identity-based habits are more sustainable
- **Favorite Quote:** "You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."

#### Designing Data-Intensive Applications
- **Author:** Martin Kleppmann
- **Finished:** 2025-09-20
- **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Genre:** Technical
- **Summary:** Deep dive into the architecture and principles behind modern data systems
- **Key Takeaways:**
  - CAP theorem and its practical implications
  - Difference between OLTP and OLAP systems
  - Stream processing vs batch processing
  - Importance of data consistency models

## 2024

### ⭐ Highly Recommended

#### Clean Code
- **Author:** Robert C. Martin
- **Finished:** 2024-11-20
- **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Genre:** Technical
- **Summary:** A handbook of agile software craftsmanship with practical advice on writing clean, maintainable code
- **Key Takeaways:**
  - Meaningful names matter more than you think
  - Functions should do one thing and do it well
  - Comments are often a code smell
  - The Boy Scout Rule: Leave code cleaner than you found it

#### Thinking, Fast and Slow
- **Author:** Daniel Kahneman
- **Finished:** 2024-08-15
- **Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Genre:** Psychology
- **Summary:** Exploration of the two systems that drive the way we think
- **Key Takeaways:**
  - System 1 (fast, intuitive) vs System 2 (slow, deliberate)
  - Cognitive biases affect our decisions
  - We're not as rational as we think
